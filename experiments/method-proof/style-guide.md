# Style Guide

- Warm off-white paper background.
- Dark ink outlines.
- Muted teal and warm coral accents.
- Flat 2D illustrated shapes.
- Camera movement only when it directs attention.
- Anticipation before the gust.
- Continuous travel during movement.
- Tilt → recover on the complication.
- Ripple → float → settle on the payoff.
- Paper boat remains one stable visual identity.
