# invisible-rules ep02 voice-over

Spoken lines stay in Chinese. Headings are English so the toolkit can score the five beats.

- **One thing:** several cashiers sharing one line means a later arrival cannot start ahead of you; separate lines, even if everyone picks shortest, still overtake about four in ten people.
- **One action:** if you see one snake to several windows, join it.
- **Number sources:** 四成, 约 4 人, 零, 三倍, 半分钟 all from `sim/results.json` / fact-card B1–B5. The “3× faster” rumor is A4.
- **Aspect and duration:** 16:9, target 80s, matches `brief.md`.
- **Cast:** 等等 (stand-in), 阿规 (rule), 嘀嗒 (one number per line). Extra people are silent silhouettes.

## Hook

Picture: eye-level three cashier lines. Dengdeng holds a full basket. On the left, a late shopper with one bottle pays and waves.

> 等等：哎？他比我晚来，怎么先走了？
> 嘀嗒：不止他。排三条队，四成的人会被后来的人超车。

## Break the assumption

Picture: Dengdeng taps his chest; headcounts appear. Agui slaps a card on “40%”: everyone picks shortest. Top-down, each person holds a closed box.

> 等等：那是他运气好。我每次都挑最短的。
> 阿规：这四成，就是人人都挑最短算出来的。
> 等等：不可能！
> 阿规：人数你看得见。前面那位要结多久，你看不准。
> 嘀嗒：被超车的人，平均被四个人超过。

## Mechanism

Picture: Agui snaps; three lines zip into one snake feeding three windows. Split: the same cashier jams — left line freezes, right snake still moves.

> 阿规：换个排法。三个收银台，只排一条队。
> 阿规：哪个台空了，队头就去哪个。
> 等等：这么长一条？我宁可排短的。
> 阿规：短队前面卡住一个人，你只能干等。
> 阿规：长龙卡住一个台，另外两个照样收人。
> 嘀嗒：一条队里，被后来的人超车的，零个。

## Payoff

Picture: “3×” hits; Dida covers his eyes; a stamp says “only if you pick blind”. The type shrinks to two bars; the gap becomes a half-minute watch.

> 等等：公平我认。可网上说长龙快三倍，真的假的？
> 嘀嗒：那得闭着眼随便挑队。
> 嘀嗒：你会挑最短的，差距就剩半分钟。
> 等等：才半分钟？那我图啥？
> 阿规：图后来的人，再也走不到你前头。
> 等等：……行。这条龙，我排。

## Audience stance

Picture: title card. Three tip cards flash, not spoken. Split A / B.

> 阿规：长龙别嫌长，它不会让你排错队。
> 等等：换你，选哪个？
> 嘀嗒：一条长龙扣 A，三条短队自己挑扣 B。
