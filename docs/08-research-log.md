# Research Log

## 2026-09-23 — Competitive Research

### Finding

The programmatic video ecosystem has mature rendering systems including Remotion, Motion Canvas, and Manim. Commercial APIs also cover JSON/template-driven video generation.

### Finding

Newer GitHub projects increasingly combine AI agents with programmatic renderers. Examples include Code2MP4, Clipwright, Vex, HyperFrames, and anything2explainer.

### Finding

Reusable motion and shot recipes are becoming explicit project assets, reducing the novelty of simply providing a motion component library.

### Implication

A new renderer or generic agent-to-video pipeline is not a sufficiently differentiated research direction.

### Hypothesis

A semantic-to-visual layer that explicitly maps content relationships to reusable visual grammar may reduce manual visual planning and improve consistency for structured knowledge videos.
