# Glossary

**Semantic Model** — structured representation of claims, entities and relationships in source content.

**Visual Grammar** — mapping from semantic relationships to visual forms and motion.

**VideoSpec** — renderer-independent machine-readable video program.

**Scene** — time-bounded visual unit with one dominant purpose.

**Deterministic Renderer** — renderer whose output is governed by explicit inputs rather than stochastic generation.

**Production Task** — a real content-generation workload used to evaluate quality, stability and cost.
