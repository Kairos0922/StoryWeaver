# Visual rhythm

Rules that survive a change of series or language.

## Picture clock

- Plan in 10-second rows with three cells: 0–3s, 3–7s, 7–10s.
- Hang picture events on a 2.5s tick.
- Fail a stretch longer than 5s with no new visual.
- At least four named devices in a finished row (kinetic number, cut, stamp, character action, compare bars, cards, push, pan).
- At least one character action per tick. Idle beside a chart fails.

## Voice clock

- Separate from the picture clock. The voice is slower.
- Chinese: ≤ 4.0 characters/s of speech; ≤ 4.6 on one line.
- English: ≤ 2.8 words/s of speech; ≤ 3.2 on one line.
- After a key number, hold ≥ 0.9s and freeze the number. Do not start a new action in that hold.
- One number per spoken line. A comparison uses a cut or two bars, not one sentence.
- Emotion cues describe tone. “Speak fast” is a lint warning.

## Hook

- 2–5 seconds. Paradox or offense first.
- At most one new term in the first 10 seconds.
- Do not follow the hook with a title card and a long morning anecdote.
- Render the first ~12 seconds and play it on a phone before you approve the rest.

## Banned picture defaults

Centered title on a gradient. Everything fading in at once. Corner labels and frame borders. Glow on UI chrome. Particle bursts. One crossfade for the whole film. A schematic sold as a measurement. Unlicensed music.
